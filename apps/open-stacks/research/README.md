# Research dossiers

These are the research notes behind the Open Stacks wiki. They are **not site content**. Each dossier was written by a research agent in October 2026 from web sources. A second, independent agent then tried to refute its load-bearing claims (prices, counts, legal facts, product statuses), corrected the text inline (marked `[corrected in verification]`), and appended a "Verification pass" table.

Treat them as raw material. Where a wiki page and a dossier disagree, the wiki page is the current position: chapters received a further fact-check pass. Prices, catalog sizes and product statuses are as of the dossier date and go stale fastest.
