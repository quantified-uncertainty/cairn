## AI safety and agent security

- **Greenblatt, R., Shlegeris, B., Sachan, K., & Roger, F. (2023).** *AI Control: Improving Safety Despite Intentional Subversion.* [arXiv:2312.06942](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06942); ICML 2024. The source of the trusted/untrusted vocabulary used here.
- **Bhatt, A., et al. (2025).** *Ctrl-Z: Controlling AI Agents via Resampling.* [arXiv:2504.10374](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10374). The first control evaluation in an agent environment.
- **Sandoval, A., & Rushing, C. (2025).** *Factor(T,U): Factored Cognition Strengthens Monitoring of Untrusted AI.* [arXiv:2512.02157](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.02157).
- **Schoen, B., et al. (2025).** *Stress Testing Deliberative Alignment for Anti-Scheming Training.* [arXiv:2509.15541](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15541).
- **Debenedetti, E., et al. (2025).** *Defeating Prompt Injections by Design* (CaMeL). [arXiv:2503.18813](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18813).
- **Beurer-Kellner, L., et al. (2025).** *Design Patterns for Securing LLM Agents against Prompt Injections.* [arXiv:2506.08837](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08837).
- **Goel, S., et al. (2025).** *Great Models Think Alike and this Undermines AI Oversight.* [arXiv:2502.04313](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04313).
- **Kim, E., Garg, A., Peng, K., & Garg, N. (2025).** *Correlated Errors in Large Language Models.* [arXiv:2506.07962](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07962).
- **Kwa, T., et al. (2025).** *Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Software Tasks.* [arXiv:2503.14499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14499).
- **Clymer, J., Gabrieli, N., Krueger, D., & Larsen, T. (2024).** *Safety Cases: How to Justify the Safety of Advanced AI Systems.* [arXiv:2403.10462](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.10462).
- **Drexler, K.E. (2019).** *Reframing Superintelligence: Comprehensive AI Services as General Intelligence.* Technical Report #2019-1, Future of Humanity Institute. [PDF](https://www.futureofhumanityinstitute.org/s/2019-1.pdf).
- **Gwern (2016).** *Why Tool AIs Want to Be Agent AIs.* [gwern.net](https://gwern.net/tool-ai).
- **Dalrymple, D., et al. (2024).** *Towards Guaranteed Safe AI.* [arXiv:2405.06624](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.06624).
- **Tegmark, M., & Omohundro, S. (2023).** *Provably Safe Systems: The Only Path to Controllable AGI.* [arXiv:2309.01933](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.01933).
- **Christiano, P., Shlegeris, B., & Amodei, D. (2018).** *Supervising Strong Learners by Amplifying Weak Experts.* [arXiv:1810.08575](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.08575).
- **Bowman, S.R., et al. (2022).** *Measuring Progress on Scalable Oversight for Large Language Models.* [arXiv:2211.03540](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.03540).
- **Bai, Y., et al. (2022).** *Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback.* [arXiv:2212.08073](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.08073).

## Reliability engineering and nuclear safety

- **Mosleh, A., Fleming, K.N., Parry, G.W., et al. (1988).** *Procedures for Treating Common Cause Failures in Safety and Reliability Studies.* NUREG/CR-4780, US Nuclear Regulatory Commission. The beta-factor and related common-cause models.
- **Knight, J.C., & Leveson, N.G. (1986).** An Experimental Evaluation of the Assumption of Independence in Multiversion Programming. *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering*, 12(1), 96–109.
- **Avizienis, A. (1985).** The N-Version Approach to Fault-Tolerant Software. *IEEE Transactions on Software Engineering.*
- **Reason, J. (1990).** *Human Error.* Cambridge University Press. The account of layered defenses later popularized as the "Swiss cheese" model.
- **Vesely, W.E., et al. (1981).** *Fault Tree Handbook.* NUREG-0492, US Nuclear Regulatory Commission. [PDF](https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML1007/ML100780465.pdf).
- **Rasmussen, N.C., et al. (1975).** *Reactor Safety Study* (WASH-1400). US Atomic Energy Commission.
- **US NRC (1990).** *Severe Accident Risks: An Assessment for Five US Nuclear Power Plants* (NUREG-1150).
- **IAEA (2006).** *Fundamental Safety Principles* (Safety Fundamentals No. SF-1).
- **ISO 26262:2018.** *Road vehicles: Functional safety.*
- **D'Ambrosio, J., & Debouk, R. (2013).** *ASIL Decomposition: The Good, the Bad, and the Ugly.* SAE Technical Paper 2013-01-0195.

## Economics: agency and mechanism design

- **Jensen, M.C., & Meckling, W.H. (1976).** Theory of the Firm: Managerial Behavior, Agency Costs and Ownership Structure. *Journal of Financial Economics*, 3(4), 305–360. The source of "agency cost".
- **Holmström, B. (1979).** Moral Hazard and Observability. *Bell Journal of Economics*, 10(1), 74–91. The informativeness principle behind monitoring.
- **Bolton, P., & Dewatripont, M. (2005).** *Contract Theory.* MIT Press.
- **Myerson, R.B. (1979).** Incentive Compatibility and the Bargaining Problem. *Econometrica*, 47(1), 61–73. The revelation principle for Bayesian settings.
- **Vickrey, W. (1961); Clarke, E.H. (1971); Groves, T. (1973).** The papers behind the VCG mechanism (*Journal of Finance*; *Public Choice*; *Econometrica*).
- **Shapley, L.S. (1953).** A Value for n-Person Games. In *Contributions to the Theory of Games II*.
