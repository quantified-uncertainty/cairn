CAIRN is the [Quantified Uncertainty Research Institute](https://quantifieduncertainty.org/)'s monorepo of research wikis. Each wiki is an Astro Starlight site, drafted and revised largely with Claude Code, and deployed to Vercel from [one repository](https://github.com/quantified-uncertainty/cairn). This handbook covers how the repository works and how its content is reviewed.
