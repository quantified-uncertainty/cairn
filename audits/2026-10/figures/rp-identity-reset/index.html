Identity and Track-Record Infrastructure · The quantity: enforceable penalty

Plug in the deterrence chapter’s numbers: a $0.01 LLM-judge call, a 5% audit rate. A newcomer with nothing escrowed has Beff ≈ 0, so S* ≈ 0.01/0.95: about one cent of safe stake. An established producer reaches the deterrence chapter’s $53 safe stake only if ΔR is about $1,000. That needs a ruin rule, a standing worth $1,000 that re-entry can’t recover, and a producer not near its last round.

So a track record works like a bond that can’t be refunded but can be spent once, and its deterrent value falls to zero as exit approaches. A mechanism that sizes stakes to standing has to cap them at what escrow alone covers, or accept that the most trusted producers are also the most tempted.
