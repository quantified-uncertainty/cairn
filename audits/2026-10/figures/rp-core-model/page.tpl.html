The Core Model

Setup

A judge J must make decisions under uncertainty. Their preferences over knowledge are summarized by a utility function U, represented in practice as a weighted question portfolio (Constructing Utility Functions). A reasoning process π — peer review, a prediction market, an LLM pipeline, an expert panel — consumes effort and evidence and emits claims, estimates, or evaluations. The judge cannot verify the claims object-level; these notes are about what they can do instead.

Three families of quantities characterize π: what it delivers, what it costs, and what it costs to corrupt. (All symbols are indexed in the Glossary & Notation.)
