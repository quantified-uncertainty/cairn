What Can You Use from an Untrustworthy Source? · Deception is a game the defender moves first in

The naive picture is a listener filtering a message. The better picture is a sequential game in which the defender moves first: the structure of Stackelberg security games. The defender commits, the attacker observes the committed environment and best-responds, and the defender’s craft is choosing the commitment that leaves the attacker the worst best response.

Call the set of routes a strategic sender could use to move you the attack surface, and each route a channel. Most of the defense is then design-time: nobody can lie past a proof checker, a neutral reproduction, or a resolving bet, and the runtime test works only on channels that survived Move 1. A running case: an AI advocate trying to nudge its overseer toward approving a plan.
