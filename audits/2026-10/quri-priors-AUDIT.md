The biggest wins come from three additions. First, a "Status / later versions" line on every draft, because readers (and LLMs) will otherwise cite a 2023 draft when a published 2023 post exists. Second, a 2026 Afterword on the ~15 drafts whose claims have been overtaken. Third, cluster pages and reading paths that connect the drafts to each other and to the three CAIRN sibling wikis that descend from them.

## 5. Proposed format for additions

**Principles:** never inside the draft's prose; always dated; always attributed (human editor, AI, or the author); visually and semantically distinct; present identically in HTML, `all-drafts.html` and `all-drafts.txt`; excluded from the draft's word count.

**Placement and components** (Starlight custom components, e.g. `src/components/EditorNote.astro` and `Afterword.astro`):

1. **Metadata aside (existing; extend it)**: Author · written date (or "c. 2019–20, est.") · tier · **Status** line: "Later published as … (date, link)" / "Became: Squiggle" / "Unpublished".
2. **Ozzie Gooen's take (existing)**: unchanged; author voice only.
3. **Opus summary (existing)**: keep, but relabel consistently ("AI summary — Claude Opus 4.8, 2026").
4. **`<EditorNote>`, above the draft (new, optional, ≤80 words).** For things a reader needs *before* reading: errata pointers, fictional-vs-real disambiguation, provenance (omitted notes, co-authorship), "this was later published, read that first". Styling: Starlight `caution`-style left border in a distinct accent colour (not the `note`/`tip` colours already used), heading "Editor's note · 2026-10 · <editor name or 'Claude Opus, reviewed by O.G.'>".
5. **Draft text**, byte-identical to source, starting after a thin labelled rule: "Draft as written (unchanged)".
6. **Restored collaborator comments (existing)**, kept where they are.
7. **`<Afterword>`, below everything (new).** Heading "Afterword (2026)", a dated byline, then up to four fixed subsections, each optional:
   - **What held up** · **What was superseded** (with verified citations only) · **Later versions & related work** (links) · **Errata & link status**.
   Styling: full-width panel with a tinted background and a "2026" date badge. Collapsible on mobile (`<details open>` on desktop). `aria-label="Afterword (2026), editorial addition"`.
8. **Related drafts box** (new): 2–4 links plus sibling-wiki links, rendered by layout (not content).

**Attribution rule:** additions written or drafted by an LLM say so in the byline ("Drafted by Claude Opus, reviewed by …"). Unreviewed AI text is labelled "unreviewed". Every external claim in an Afterword carries a link; no citation is added without a working source.

**Export rule:** in `all-drafts.txt`/`.md`, additions appear after the draft between `---- Editorial additions (not part of the original draft) ----` and `---- end additions ----`, so tooling can strip them and recover the exact original.
