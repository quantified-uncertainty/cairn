# Homepage brief, revised after owner feedback (supersedes the styling parts of FAMILY.md)

## What the owner said (2026-10-15, verbatim in substance)

- Of the first two Handbook concepts, the editorial one was better, but both are "a bit ugly" and "trying to be too fancy".
- "Carried away with fancy fonts and lots of other stuff. It's harder to read."
- **"The key thing is readability."** Make it "a fair bit more minimalistic".
- A few very clever diagrams here and there are welcome. Decoration is not.
- It "shouldn't give the impression like this is some super fancy web app". These are straightforward, simple sites.
- This applies above all to the CAIRN Handbook, which is tiny (about 6 pages).

## Rules

1. **Readability first.**
   - Body text is 16–18px, line-height about 1.6, measure 60–75 characters, with strong contrast.
   - One readable text face for everything. Starlight's default system stack is acceptable.
   - At most one other face, used only for the page title, and only if it clearly improves the page.
   - No italic paragraphs.
   - No monospace for labels, kickers or metadata. Monospace is for code only.
   - No ALL-CAPS letter-spaced eyebrows.
2. **No ornament.** Leave all of these out:
   - contour, grid or paper backgrounds; hatching; textures; drop caps;
   - "§" numerals; masthead rules; "Fig. 0" plates with double borders;
   - decorative glyph icons on every card;
   - animated strokes;
   - multiple accent colours on one page.
3. **Not an app.**
   - No big button rows, pill CTAs, cards-with-icons grids, "you are here" badges or stats strips ("5 pages · 4 sites · 38 min").
   - Links are ordinary links. One clear "start here" link is enough.
4. **At most one diagram on a homepage, and only if it earns its place.**
   - It shows the site's central mechanism in a way a sentence cannot.
   - It is drawn in thin lines, labelled in plain text at body size, uses the single accent at most once, and is correct.
   - If it doesn't clearly help, leave it out. The Handbook probably needs none, or at most a tiny inline cairn mark.
5. **Per-site identity is small.** One accent colour, used for links and the one highlighted element in the diagram. Otherwise the four sites look the same.
6. **Keep Starlight's chrome:** header, search and theme toggle. The work is the content area of the splash page plus light CSS, so it's cheap to implement and maintain.
7. **Content structure on the homepage:**
   - Title.
   - One or two sentences saying what the site is and its epistemic status (plain text, not an alert box).
   - Where to start: 1–3 links.
   - A short, scannable contents list. A plain list or a compact table of chapters, each with a one-line description.
   - Sibling wikis as one plain line of links.
   - QURI credit.
   - Everything stays short. The Handbook homepage should fit on about one screen.
8. **Both themes and 375px width must work.** Check contrast. Neutral greys are fine; FAMILY.md's warm neutrals may be kept if they stay subtle.

## Reference points

Think a well-set personal research site or a plain, excellent README, not a magazine and not a product page. Examples: Gwern-style restraint without the density, Distill without the flash, a Tufte-ish preference for content over chrome.

## Revision 2: owner feedback on the minimal finals (2026-10-15)

"Decent, but you went a little too far." The target is now about **90% the minimal finals, 10% flourish**:

- **Small design flourishes are welcome.** For example, a very light background treatment, or one basic, interesting typeface that stays easy to read.
- **Diagrams should be more sophisticated than the round-2 ones.** They should still be clean and correct, but they should carry more real information and insight.
- Everything in rules 1–8 above still applies, except as relaxed here:
  - One distinctive but readable face is allowed, for headings or the title.
  - A subtle background treatment is allowed.
  - Each site may have one diagram.
