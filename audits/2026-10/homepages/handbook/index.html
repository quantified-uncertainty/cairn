CAIRN Handbook

This handbook explains how QURI builds and maintains CAIRN, its repository of research wikis, as of October 2026. The wikis are drafted largely with Claude Code and checked in review rounds that treat model-written text as claims to verify.

Start with the Review Workflow, the five-stage round used to check the wikis. To build a site of your own from an empty folder, take the Workshop.
