Robust Reasoning Processes

Notes on measuring reasoning processes such as peer review, prediction markets, audits and LLM judges: what they deliver, what they cost, and what it costs to corrupt them. This is an early research exploration, with drafts and conjectures rather than settled results.

Start with Chapter 1, or go straight to The Process Catalogue.

The map

Each process gets two numbers: what it costs to run, per bit of validated information, and what an adversary must pay to bend its output. Plotting the catalogue’s 17 processes on those two axes leaves one corner empty.
